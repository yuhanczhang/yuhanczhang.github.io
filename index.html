I am a postdoctoral scholar in the Department of Linguistics at Stanford University, affiliated with Stanford BIO-X. I am interested in computational psycholinguistics and language neuroscience, with a focus on understanding the cognitive representations and computation that enable the human brain to make sense of the world via language in an efficient way, and how this perspective helps us better understand the nature of computational language models.

Recent Projects

  • Linguistic Illusions Depth-charge illusion, Comparative illusion
  • Models of language processing Bayesian modeling and acceptability, LLMs with illusion
  • Neuroimaging (fMRI) Bayesian inference of language illusion in the brain
